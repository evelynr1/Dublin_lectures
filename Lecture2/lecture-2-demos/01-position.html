Demo 01 — position values

Five values, one page. Read each section and play with the values in DevTools.

1. static — default

The element ignores top/right/bottom/left. It just lives where the flow puts it.

I am static. top: 100px would be ignored.

2. relative — keeps its slot, nudges from there

Element is offset visually but its original space is preserved.

Sibling above (static)
I am position: relative; top: 8px; left: 16px;
Sibling below — notice the relative one didn't push me anywhere.

3. absolute — pinned to a positioned ancestor

The .badge is positioned relative to .product-card because the card has position: relative.

Sale Postcards from Galway

Pack of 12. Hand-written option available.

4. fixed — anchored to the viewport

Look at the bottom-right corner. Scroll up and down — the button stays put.

5. sticky — relative until it hits top: 0

Scroll down. The blue header above sticks to the top of the viewport until this whole section scrolls past, then it lets go.

Sticky needs an offset (top: 0) and a scrollable parent — the page itself counts. A common gotcha is putting overflow: hidden on a parent, which silently breaks sticky.
